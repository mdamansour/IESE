# Master IESE - Student Resources

Welcome! This repository is a curated collection of resources and preparation materials for students in the Master IESE program in alternance with ALTEN. It brings together course notes, TDs, TPs, exam prep, and assorted references gathered along the way, so that future students can start with a clearer path and stronger support.

I created this because I have been in your shoes. I know the pace can be intense and the workload can feel heavy at times. My goal is to make your journey a bit smoother by sharing what helped me and my classmates.

## Who this is for

- Current or incoming Master IESE students (alternance with ALTEN)
- Anyone preparing for the same modules and evaluations
- Students looking for extra practice, references, and guidance

## What you will find here

- Course materials
- TDs, TPs, and reports
- Past exams, controls, and corrections (when available)
- Tools and utilities used during the program
- Extra notes and external references

## How to use this repository

- Browse by year and semester under `Ressources/`
- Open the folders for your module to find Cours, TDs, TPs, and archives
- Use the search in your editor to locate specific topics or file names
- Feel free to adapt the materials to your learning style

## A friendly note

If you are just starting out, please remember: be patient, stay consistent, and keep working hard. Progress builds over time, even if it feels slow at first. You can do this, and you do not have to do it alone.

I truly wish you all the best in your studies and your alternance. If you ever need help or just want to ask a question, you can always reach me on LinkedIn.
